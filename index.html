How It All Comes Together

Three phases of raw power — from bare chassis to creative dominance

01
Phase One — Foundation

Platform & Power

  • Helios GX601 chassis provides the structural and thermal skeleton — four 140mm fans, 420mm front rad mount, GPU sag bracket system.
  • ROG Thor 1200W seats in the PSU shroud. Its LiveDash OLED will display real-time wattage the moment the system POSTs.
  • Kraken Elite 420 mounts front-intake. Three F140 RGB fans push through the radiator toward the rear exhaust path.
  • X870E Hero drops into the ATX standoffs. M.2 slots pre-populated before mounting — no disassembly later.
Helios GX601 ROG Thor 1200W Kraken Elite 420 X870E Hero
02
Phase Two — Silicon

Compute Core

  • 9950X3D lands on AM5 with zero insertion force. Pump head locks direct onto the IHS — no contact frame required on Zen 5 dies.
  • 96GB Vengeance DDR5 in A2+B2 slots. System POSTs to 4800MHz JEDEC; one BIOS toggle enables 6000MHz EXPO.
  • Samsung 9100 Pro 8TB slides into the M2_1 PCIe 5.0 slot. 14.8 GB/s sequential read is immediately available post-format.
  • EPS 8-pin CPU cable from Thor routed behind the motherboard tray — clean, zero-obstruction airflow to the Kraken radiator.
Ryzen 9 9950X3D 96GB DDR5-6000 9100 Pro 8TB
03
Phase Three — Vision

Graphics & Display

  • TUF RTX 5080 OC occupies the PCIe 5.0 x16 SafeSlot. The GPU sag bracket clips to the Helios mounting rail — no flex on the 3.6-slot card.
  • 16-pin 600W cable from Thor connects directly. At peak OC the card draws ~320W — the Thor barely sweats at 26% of rated capacity.
  • Odyssey OLED G9 connects via DisplayPort 2.1. Driver install enables 5120×1440 @ 240Hz with G-Sync Compatible and HDR True Black 400.
  • Full system: 9 components, one ecosystem. Aura Sync, iCUE, NZXT CAM, and Armoury Crate unify lighting across every RGB element.
RTX 5080 OC Odyssey OLED G9 Aura Sync
